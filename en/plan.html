1. Developing the command-support tool

We develop CommandClock, a support tool used by commanders and teammates in mobile strategy games. With a single call, it tells each teammate their own moment to press, with a countdown. It’s available in a browser and as an Android app.

2. Selling the tool

CommandClock is offered on monthly plans.

  • Alliance Plan: 1 alliance, 2 squads. No limit on people or devices
  • Commander Plan: includes the console. 3 alliances, 6 squads. No limit on people or devices

The first 14 days are free. For prices and features, see the pricing on the product page.
